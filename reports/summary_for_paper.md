Task 1. The continuous-observation domain contained 4,072,765 of 9,379,006 reference-mask cells (43.42%). Under the documented cartographic Qinling–Huaihe partition, 82.73% of retained cells occurred north of the line. The arid, semi-arid, dry-subhumid and humid zones contained 1531127, 1194602, 485077, 861959 retained cells, respectively. Coverage is spatially selective; the north–south percentage is conditional on the published map line and its explicitly documented endpoint extensions.

Task 2. On the same 4,072,765 cells, holding P and ET0 at their 2000–2025 means changed the 2020–2025 regional EHI change from -2.732% to 1.245%, and reduced amplitude-screened mismatch to 1.872%. The difference between observed and fixed-weather EHI changes was -0.001176 EHI, equivalent to 139.6% of the observed decline, because the remaining change was positive. WY was the dominant negative channel in 0.48% of the remaining screened-mismatch cells. This controlled model comparison describes dependence on direct WY weather inputs and does not identify a causal climate effect.

Task 3. Replacing the FP reference min–max range with the pooled-reference-year 2nd–98th percentiles yielded bounds of 0–4.029 t ha⁻¹ of whole-cell area, a 2020–2025 EHI change of 0.8261%, and amplitude-screened mismatch of 11.49% across 4,072,765 cells. These results quantify sensitivity to the FP scaling convention; FP is an area-allocated service measure, not crop yield.

Task 4. With separately refitted WY reference bounds, the 2020–2025 EHI changes for Z = 1, 5, 10 and 20 were -2.732%, -5.129%, -6.375%, -7.054%, respectively, on 4,072,765 cells. Their corresponding main-domain WY changes were -26.34 mm, -39.18 mm, -44.82 mm, -47.36 mm. This comparison extends the existing full-primary-domain WY sensitivity to EHI with refitted WY bounds; the matched GLDAS subset remains a separate support.

Task 5. Hamed–Rao modified Mann–Kendall tests with all available autocorrelation lags and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control at q = 0.05 classified 62.17% of cells as significantly increasing, 1.957% as significantly decreasing, and 34.69% as nonsignificant; 48375 cells had undefined corrected tests. The regional mean series had a Sen slope of 0.0002511 EHI yr⁻¹ and a modified-MK p value of 1.974e-07. The correction addresses serial dependence in each time series, while FDR is applied jointly to the primary-domain pixel tests.

Task 6. Across 1,000 Dirichlet weight draws with alpha = (5, 5, 5, 5), total concentration 20 and random seed 20261109, the proportions of negative EHI changes for 2000–2025 and 2020–2025 were 0%, 98.2%, with respective 5th–95th percentile change ranges of 15.96% to 46.77%; -5.482% to -0.453%. All draws retained the same 4,072,765 cells; these ranges describe sensitivity to weights rather than sampling confidence intervals.
